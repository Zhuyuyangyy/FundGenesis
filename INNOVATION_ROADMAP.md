1. A four-tier KOL social network model for narrative propagation
2. A five-factor belief update engine (inertia, narrative, price confirmation, social pressure, contradiction)

4. Reflexivity-aware metrics for real-time market state assessment

**Key Claims**:
- Causal chain enforcement: Narrative → Belief → Emotion → Behavior → Capital → Price
- Agent-specific belief modulation based on emotional sensitivity and herding coefficient
- Trust bootstrapping and falsification-triggered collapse mechanisms
- Composite reflexivity index computation

**Commercial Applications**:

- Regulatory sandbox simulations

**Objective**: Extend the framework to model reflexivity across multiple markets (stocks, bonds, commodities, crypto).

**Approach**:
- Multi-asset KOL networks with cross-market narrative propagation
- Correlation-aware belief update with cross-asset confirmation
- Systemic risk metrics for multi-market reflexivity

**Expected Outcomes**:
- Cross-market contagion simulation capability
- Systemic risk early warning system
- Multi-asset portfolio optimization with reflexivity awareness

**Objective**: Implement learning mechanisms for agents to adapt their strategies over time.

**Approach**:

**Expected Outcomes**:

- Validation against real market data

**Approach**:

**Expected Outcomes**:
